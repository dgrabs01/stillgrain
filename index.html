What it does

Made as you listen

White, pink and brown noise and soft tones, generated in the moment. Nothing repeats, because nothing is recorded.

One orb to shape it

Left and right make the sound darker or brighter. Up adds more tone. You can feel each step.

Headphones or speaker

Binaural beats for headphones, gentle pulses for the speaker. Named honestly, with no promises about your brain.

Tinnitus masking tools

Find the pitch you hear, then play soft noise with a gap around it. Designed for masking; not a treatment.

A timer that fades out

From 15 minutes to 8 hours. At the end the sound fades to silence, then stops.

Offline and private

No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Works the same in Airplane Mode. The screen dims itself at your bedside.

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026

Stillgrain does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings and saved presets, stored on your iPhone only (not synced to iCloud).
  • A small snapshot of the current preset and playback state, shared with Stillgrain's own Home Screen widgets.

I bought Lifetime but features are locked

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases while online. You must use the same Apple Account you bought with.

I can't hear the binaural beat

Binaural beats need headphones: each ear gets a slightly different tone. On the speaker, choose Speaker in Settings to use gentle pulses instead.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All sound is generated on the device. Only the App Store purchase needs a connection.

Why did playback pause?

By default Stillgrain pauses at once when headphones disconnect, so sound doesn't suddenly play from the speaker. You can turn this off in Settings.
